Complete Guide to First-Attack Strategy Revelation & All 6 Modes

1. Why 64-Token Chessformer (Maia-3) + GAB Is Selected
Traditional engines (Stockfish/Leela) plateau at 46.0% human move prediction accuracy and lose predictive accuracy when tree search is enabled. Our engine combines the searchless 64-square-token Chessformer trunk with dynamic Geometric Attention Bias (smolgen), a Source-Destination policy head, and clock-trace conditioning to reach 57.1% Top-1 human move accuracy.
2. First-Attack Strategy Revelation & GenBR Counter-Synthesis
During Plies 1–10, every opponent move and clock duration updates the recursive Bayesian belief distribution B_{t+1}(\theta_i) and Bayes-Relational prior ratio. Once locked, the Policy-Value-and-Generative Network (PVGN) and Information Set MCTS synthesize an exploitative counter-strategy regularized by a Level-5 Deep Cognitive Hierarchy (56.1% JPC loss reduction).
3. 172-Concept Linear Probe Oracle & Dynamic Spatial Influence Maps
The 1024-D residual stream is probed across 172 binary chess concepts (covering 93.6% of transitional blunders). When the 2600-Elo Master probe fires (>0.85) while the opponent's inferred skill probe stays dormant (<0.30), the exact blind spot is highlighted on the board alongside directionally biased dynamic influence shaders.
